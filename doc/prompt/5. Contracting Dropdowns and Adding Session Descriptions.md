### Planner Response

The fix uses `overflow: clip` instead of `overflow: hidden` on the accordion content containers. Here's why this works:

- **`overflow: hidden`** creates a scroll container, which clips *all* descendants — including absolutely positioned ones regardless of their `z-index`.
- **`overflow: clip`** clips layout overflow for the animation (so the collapsing still works visually), but **does not create a scroll container** — meaning absolutely positioned children like `.lectures-count` can paint outside the box boundaries and show in front without being clipped.

The collapse/expand `max-height` animation still works exactly the same, but the count badge on `.course-accordion-header` will no longer be swallowed by the parent `.root-accordion-content`.
